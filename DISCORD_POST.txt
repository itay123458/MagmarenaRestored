**Magmarena Restored | v0.1.0**

Brings back the old Magmarena through Forest, with the original ball game, teams, goals, and scoreboards. Includes fixes for the tunnel and broken arena lighting.

- Private rooms and modded private rooms only.
- PC/Steam Gorilla Tag build 26061329 + BepInEx 5.
- Everyone playing needs the mod, including the host. PUN rooms only.
- Tested with a friend in a private room.

**Install:** Close Gorilla Tag and extract the release ZIP into the game folder. The DLL goes in BepInEx/plugins/MagmarenaPrivate/. Then join a private room and use the entrance in Forest.
